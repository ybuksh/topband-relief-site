Our fees

v1.0 · Last updated November 2026

You can do this yourself for free. You can apply to HMRC yourself through GOV.UK and keep all of any money you're owed. You can also contact HMRC or DWP yourself at any time. Our service is optional.

What we charge

  • Our fee is 30% of the backdated arrears actually paid to you, plus VAT at 20%. In total you pay 36% of the arrears you receive.
  • There is no fee on any increase to your weekly State Pension or Pension Credit.
  • If DWP keeps back part of your arrears, for example to repay a benefit overpayment, we only charge on the amount actually paid to you.
  • If no arrears are paid, you pay nothing, even if your National Insurance record is corrected. There is nothing to pay upfront.
Example
Arrears in the DWP letter£2,000.00
Kept back by DWP to repay a debt−£300.00
Paid to you£1,700.00
Our fee (30%)£510.00
VAT (20% of our fee)£102.00
You keep£1,088.00

When you pay

DWP pays any arrears straight to you; we never receive your pension or arrears. Please tell us within 14 days of receiving arrears and send us the DWP letter showing the amount (a photo is fine). We only invoice you once the arrears have been confirmed. The invoice shows the DWP figures and how the fee is worked out, and payment is due 14 days from the invoice date.

How you can pay

  • Secure online payment link (card or bank)
  • Bank transfer
  • Card over the phone
  • Cheque by post
  • A payment plan if you need more time: just ask

If you don't use email or the internet, we post everything to you and help by phone.

Cancelling

You can cancel within 14 days of signing for any reason and pay nothing, even if we have already started work. After that you can still end the agreement at any time; we only charge if arrears are later paid as a result of a claim we had already sent to HMRC before you told us.

If you can't pay or disagree

If an invoice isn't paid we send reminders and talk to you about options such as a payment plan. Formal recovery is only ever a last resort, taking your circumstances into account. If you disagree with an invoice, tell us within 28 days; we won't chase payment while a complaint is open.
