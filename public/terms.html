These terms summarise the agreement you sign with Tax Magpie Ltd (trading as Every Penny Back). The signed client agreement is the full contract; if anything here differs, the signed agreement applies.

You can do this yourself for free. You can apply to HMRC yourself through GOV.UK and keep all of any money you're owed. You can also contact HMRC or DWP yourself at any time. Our service is optional.

1. Who we are

Tax Magpie Ltd is an independent company registered with HMRC as a tax agent. We are not part of HMRC, DWP or the government, and we are not endorsed by them. We are not a law firm. We do not act as your attorney, appointee or deputy and we never sign anything for you.

2. What we do

  • Check whether your State Pension may have been underpaid because Home Responsibilities Protection (HRP) is missing from your National Insurance record, and check for other State Pension underpayments (NI credit errors; increases based on a spouse's or civil partner's record, including Category BL; widow's, widower's or inherited pension; deferred pension; linked Pension Credit).
  • Use your answers about the years you received Child Benefit, and the years you worked full time, to choose only the tax years that need checking. We ask HMRC about those years only, never your whole record from 1978 to 2010.
  • Ask DWP for information about your State Pension, using the letter of authority you sign.
  • Review the HMRC and DWP evidence together. Only if it supports a claim do we prepare the HRP claim form (CF411), which you see in full and sign then, not at the start.
  • Send the claim, chase HMRC and DWP, and keep you updated until there is a decision.

3. What we can't promise

Receiving Child Benefit does not automatically mean you are owed anything, and does not mean a claim will be made. Only HMRC and DWP decide whether your record is corrected and what you are owed. We cannot guarantee an outcome or timescale.

5. Identity checks

We confirm who you are before any substantive work, using an electronic identity check. This may leave a "soft search" on your credit file, which lenders can't see and which doesn't affect your score.

6. Fees and payment

See our fees: 30% plus VAT (36% in total) of arrears actually paid to you, invoiced only after the arrears are confirmed, payable 14 days from the invoice date. No fee on weekly increases. Nothing to pay if no arrears are paid.

7. Cancelling

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you can cancel within 14 days of signing and pay nothing, even if work has started. Call 0161 399 2916, email hello@everypennyback.co.uk, write to us, or reply to any of our messages.

8. What we need from you

Accurate information, letters you receive from HMRC or DWP about your record or pension (especially any arrears letter), and telling us if anyone else is already helping you.

9. Tax and benefits

State Pension arrears are taxable income, and a higher State Pension can affect means-tested benefits such as Pension Credit or Housing Benefit. Citizens Advice and Age UK give free benefits advice.

10. People who have died, and powers of attorney

A personal representative can claim arrears owed to someone who has died, with the death certificate and grant of probate or letters of administration. If someone holds a power of attorney for you, tell us before you sign.
