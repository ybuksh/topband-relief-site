Privacy notice

v1.0 · Last updated November 2026

This explains how Tax Magpie Ltd (trading as Every Penny Back) uses your personal information. We are the data controller. Contact: privacy@everypennyback.co.uk, 56 Thornbury Road, Stretford, Manchester M32 0QE.

What we collect

  • Your name, previous names, date of birth, address, phone, email and National Insurance number.
  • The years you received Child Benefit, your children's names and dates of birth, your caring and work history, and details of your partner or the other parent where you know them.
  • Identity check results, signed documents and the electronic signing record (signer name, date, time, IP address, device and document fingerprint).
  • Letters and records from HMRC and DWP about your National Insurance record and State Pension, and payment records for our fee.
  • If you are helping someone, or acting for someone who has died, your details and your relationship to them.

Who we share it with

HMRC and DWP, for your claim only; our identity-checking, e-signature, messaging, hosting and payment providers, under contracts that protect your information. We never sell your information or pass it to other companies for their marketing.

How long we keep it

Six years from the later of the date your claim closes and the date any arrears are paid, then it is securely deleted. If you only use our online check and don't go ahead, we delete your answers within 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent for marketing. Email privacy@everypennyback.co.uk. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Security

Your information is stored encrypted on UK or EU servers with access limited to staff who need it. Signed documents are sealed so that any change can be detected.
