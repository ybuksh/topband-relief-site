Questions

Common questions

Who is likely to be owed relief?

People who pay tax at 40% or 45% and pay into a personal pension, a SIPP, or a workplace pension taken from take-home pay (relief at source). If your contributions come out of your pay before tax, or through salary sacrifice, you already get full relief.

How far back can you claim?

Up to four tax years. Relief for the oldest year expires at the end of each tax year, so it's worth checking sooner rather than later.

How is the money paid?

HMRC pays you directly, usually as a refund for past years. For the current year it often adjusts your tax code instead. We never receive your money.

What do you need from me?

Photo ID, your National Insurance number, your pension provider details and your signed authority for us to act for you. We request the statements HMRC requires for each year.

How long does it take?

HMRC aims to respond within 28 working days of receiving a claim. Gathering statements from providers usually adds a few weeks.

Are you part of HMRC?

No. We are an independent company acting as your agent. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by HMRC.
